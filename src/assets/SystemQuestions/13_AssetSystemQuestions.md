System Number: 13
System Name: Asset System
Capability: Manage Information
System Scope: Defines how reusable organizational assets are organized, maintained, protected, and made accessible.

## 1. Assets

- What is the name of the asset?
- What is the asset type?
- What is the asset used for?
- Where can the asset be found?
- Who is responsible for maintaining the asset?
- What is the priority?
- What is the status?

## 2. Access

- What are the related assets?
- What is the access level?
- What limitations apply to access or use?
- What additional information should be noted about access?

## 3. Updates

- What are the related assets?
- What was reviewed, changed, or updated?
- When was the update completed?
- What is the status?

## 4. Assets Summary

- What are the most important assets?
- Which assets are currently in use?
- Which assets require attention or review?
- What is the most important insight about the asset inventory?
- When should the assets be reviewed again?
