System Number: 8
System Name: Operations System
Capability: Deliver Value
System Scope: Defines how everyday activities are organized, executed, and improved to deliver consistent operational performance.

## 1. Handbook

- Explain why the organization operates the way it does.
- Describe how work is organized.
- Record important operational rules and expectations.

## 2. Process Library

- For each process, record its name, purpose, who does it, how often it happens, and its status.
- A process can hold several SOPs, each broken down into its own ordered Steps -- a process with
  zero or one SOP shows its Steps directly; the SOP breakdown only appears once a second SOP exists.

## 3. Operational Log

- For each log entry, record date, type, description, reason, and outcome.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- For each responsibility, record activity, responsible person or team, and notes.
