System Number: 19
System Name: Publishing System
Capability: Communicate
System Scope: Defines how organizational information is prepared, published, maintained, and communicated consistently.

## 1. Content Strategy

- What should the content achieve?
- What types of content will be created?
- Who is the content intended for?
- How will success be evaluated?

## 2. Content Ideas

- For each idea, record title, category, notes, and status.

## 3. Editorial Calendar

- For each content item, record title, publication date, category, platform, and status.

## 4. Article Template

- Define the article template structure, including title, introduction, main content, conclusion, and optional references.

## 5. Review Workflow

- Define the review areas.
- Describe the approval process before publication.

## 6. Distribution Checklist

- Confirm content published, formatting verified, and links verified.
- Confirm website updated, newsletter sent, and social media shared.
- Confirm analytics enabled and results recorded.
