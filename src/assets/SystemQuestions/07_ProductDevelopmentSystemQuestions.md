System Number: 7
System Name: Product Development System
Capability: Deliver Value
System Scope: Defines how products and services are designed, developed, maintained, and continuously improved.

## 1. Product Register

- For each product, record product name, category, status, and launch date.

## 2. Product Validation

- What problem does the product solve?
- What evidence suggests people want this product?
- What alternatives already exist?
- Does the product support the organization's goals and priorities?
- What is the decision?

## 3. Product Brief

- What is the product?
- Who is the product for?
- What problem does the product solve?
- Why should customers choose this product?
- How will success be measured?

## 4. Product Specification

- List the core features.
- Define the key requirements.
- List the expected outputs.
- Identify required tools, resources, or external factors.
- Identify limitations, restrictions, or requirements.

## 5. Product Roadmap

- Define the product roadmap phases, including time period, focus, and deliverables for validation, development, testing, and launch.

## 6. Product Launch Checklist

- Confirm product completed, testing completed, and documentation completed.
- Confirm product description completed, product images prepared, and launch content prepared.
- Confirm pricing defined, sales platform ready, and payment processing active.
- Confirm whether the product is ready for release.

## 7. Product Review

- How did the product perform?
- What feedback was received?
- What worked well?
- What problems occurred?
- What should be improved next time?
- What happens next?
