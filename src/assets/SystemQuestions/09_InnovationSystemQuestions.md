System Number: 9
System Name: Innovation System
Capability: Deliver Value
System Scope: Defines how opportunities, ideas, and improvements are explored, evaluated, and transformed into organizational value.

## 1. Ideas

- What is the idea?
- What is a brief description of the idea?
- What benefit could it provide?
- What is the status?

## 2. Evaluation

- What is the related idea?
- What was discovered during evaluation?
- What is the feasibility?
- What is the potential impact?
- What is the implementation effort?
- What is the recommendation?

## 3. Actions

- What should happen next?
- Which idea does this action support?
- What is the priority?
- What is the status?

## 4. Summary

- What are the most promising ideas?
- What are the most important conclusions from evaluations?
- What are the most important next steps?
- What is the most important innovation insight?
- When should innovation activities be reviewed again?
