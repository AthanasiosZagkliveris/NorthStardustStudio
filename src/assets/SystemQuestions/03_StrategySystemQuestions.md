System Number: 3
System Name: Strategy System
Capability: Foundation
System Scope: Defines the long-term direction, priorities, objectives, and strategic choices that guide organizational development.

## 1. Current Situation Analysis

- Describe the current situation, including organization size, products or services, audience, resources, capabilities, and challenges.
- Identify opportunities currently available.
- Identify major challenges or limitations.

## 2. SWOT Analysis

- List internal strengths.
- List internal weaknesses.
- List external opportunities.
- List external threats.

## 3. Strategic Compass

- Describe the desired future direction.
- List the areas that deserve the highest attention.
- Document the assumptions that support the current strategy.
- List the criteria used when making strategic decisions.

## 4. Strategic Themes

- Define the major strategic themes for the planning period.

## 5. Goals

- Define the goals and desired outcomes.

## 6. Objectives

- Define the objectives, including related goal, description, and target date.

## 7. Key Initiatives

- Define the key initiatives, including related objective, description, owner, and status.

## 8. Success Metrics

- Define the success metrics, including description, target, and current value.

## 9. Strategic Roadmap

- Define the strategic roadmap phases, including time period, focus, and key deliverables.
