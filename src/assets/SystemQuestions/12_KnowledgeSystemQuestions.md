System Number: 12
System Name: Knowledge System
Capability: Manage Information
System Scope: Defines how organizational knowledge is captured, shared, preserved, and reused over time.

## 1. Knowledge Base

- What subject does this knowledge relate to?
- What is known about the topic?
- What is the category?
- How has the knowledge been verified?
- When should the knowledge be reviewed again?
- What is the status?

## 2. Lessons Learned

- What was learned?
- Where or when was it learned?
- How should this lesson be applied in the future?

## 3. Best Practices

- What should be done?
- Why is this considered a best practice?
- Which topic does it support?

## 4. FAQs

- What is the common question?
- What is the recommended answer?
- Which topic does this FAQ relate to?

## 5. Knowledge Summary

- What is the most important information?
- What are the most important lessons learned?
- What are the most important best practices?
- What are the most useful questions and answers?
- When should this knowledge be reviewed or updated?
