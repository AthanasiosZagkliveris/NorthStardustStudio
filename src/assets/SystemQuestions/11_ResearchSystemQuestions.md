System Number: 11
System Name: Research System
Capability: Manage Information
System Scope: Defines how information is gathered, evaluated, and applied to support informed organizational decisions.

## 1. Research Overview

- What is the name of the research?
- What is the research type?
- What is a brief description of the research?
- What is the research trying to learn, understand, or validate?

## 2. Research Questions

- What needs to be answered?
- What is the priority?
- What is the status?

## 3. Sources

- What is the source name?
- What is the source type?
- What is the reference, link, citation, or location?
- What relevant information was collected from the source?

## 4. Findings

- What information was discovered?
- Which sources support this finding?
- What is the confidence level?

## 5. Analysis

- What pattern, trend, relationship, or issue was identified?
- What does the observation mean?
- Which findings support this analysis?
- What is the confidence level?

## 6. Actions

- What should happen next?
- Why is this action recommended?
- What is the priority?
- What is the status?

## 7. Research Summary

- What are the most important discoveries?
- What are the most important conclusions from the analysis?
- What are the most important recommended actions?
- What is the single most important thing learned from the research?
- What should happen after the research is completed?
