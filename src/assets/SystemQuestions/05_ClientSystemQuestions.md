System Number: 5
System Name: Client System
Capability: Deliver Value
System Scope: Defines who the organization serves, what they need, and how lasting customer relationships are developed.

## 1. Clients

- Provide the client name.
- Identify the client type.
- Provide a brief description of the client.
- Provide the primary contact person or contact information.
- Identify the client status.

## 2. Requests

- Identify the related client.
- State what the client has requested.
- Identify the priority.
- Identify the status.

## 3. Services

- Identify the related client.
- Identify the service being provided.
- Identify the associated project, if applicable.
- Identify the status.

## 4. Relationships

- Identify the related client.
- Identify the relationship type.
- Provide important information about the relationship.
- Identify the relationship status.

## 5. Summary

- Identify clients currently being served.
- Identify the most important client requests.
- Identify services currently being provided.
- Identify the most important client relationships.
- State the most important insight about current client activity.
- Identify when client information should be reviewed again.
