System Number: 4
System Name: Business System
Capability: Foundation
System Scope: Defines how the organization creates, delivers, and sustains value over time.

## 1. Business Plan

- Describe the organization and its primary activities.
- Summarize the main products and services offered.
- Summarize the primary customer groups.
- Summarize how revenue is generated.
- Summarize current business priorities.

## 2. Business Model

- What value is provided?
- Who receives that value?
- How is value delivered?
- What resources support the business?
- What activities are required?
- What external relationships are important?

## 3. Revenue Model

- Identify the primary revenue methods.
- Estimate the revenue mix contribution of each method.

## 4. Revenue Streams

- Describe each revenue stream.

## 5. Product Register

- For each product, record name, category, status, target customer, and revenue stream.

## 6. Customer Profiles

- Define each customer group, including description, needs, challenges, and goals.

## 7. Competitor Analysis

- Define each competitor, including description, strengths, weaknesses, and differentiators.
- Describe how the organization differs from competitors.

## 8. Pricing Strategy

- Identify the pricing approach.
- List pricing rules and considerations.
- Describe pricing tiers, if applicable.

## 9. Revenue Roadmap

- Define the revenue roadmap phases, including time period, focus, and expected revenue sources.
