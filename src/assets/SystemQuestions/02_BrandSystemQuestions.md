System Number: 2
System Name: Brand System
Capability: Foundation
System Scope: Defines how the organization expresses its identity consistently across every audience, channel, and experience.
Guiding Question: How do people see, hear and recognize you?
Uses From Earlier: Nothing is carried over — it expresses the identity you set out in 1. Identity System.
Thinking Time: About 45 minutes–1 hour.

## 1. Ambassador

### Aesthetics

- Imagine your brand as a person: your Ambassador.
- What does the Ambassador look like?
- Attire, colours, grooming, scent, style, surroundings.

### Attitude & Body Language

- How does the Ambassador stand and move?
- How does the Ambassador behave?
- Posture, facial expressions, gestures, personal space.

### Communication & Voice

- How does the Ambassador sound?
- Pitch, volume, pace, tone.
- Which words and sentence style does the Ambassador use?

### Imprint

- What first impression does the Ambassador leave?
- What lasting impression remains?
- What should people feel and remember?
