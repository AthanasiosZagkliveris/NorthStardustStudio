# 21. Social Media System Help

## 01. Audience Profiles Help

Audience Profiles identifies the specific groups of people you want to reach on social media.
For each group, capture their name, description, interests, goals, and the challenges they face.
Keep groups distinct even when they overlap: separate "Entertainment Fans" from "Collectors," for instance, because they want different things from your content.
Understanding these differences is what lets your content actually speak to each group.

## 02. Platform Strategy Help

Platform Strategy defines why you're actually on each platform and what you post there.
For each platform, record its purpose, primary audience, and content focus.
Give each platform a distinct job (Instagram for visual storytelling, LinkedIn for corporate communication, TikTok for younger audiences).
This is what keeps your presence on each platform intentional.

## 03. Content Types Help

Content Types defines the different kinds of posts you create and how each one should be used.
List your content categories (announcements, educational content, behind the scenes, and similar) and set guidelines for each.
Make guidelines actionable, like "adapt content for each platform" and "maintain accessibility standards."
Clear categories and guidelines are what keep your social presence recognizable even as different people create content over time.

## 04. Posting Calendar Help

The Posting Calendar turns your content plan into an actual publishing schedule, so posting stays consistent.
For each item, set the date, platform, content type, and status (planned, scheduled, published).
Spread different content types across the week and across platforms.
This is what keeps your social media presence active and predictable for your audience.

## 05. Bio Templates Help

Bio Templates gives you ready-to-use profile descriptions at different lengths, so you're never rewriting your bio under pressure for a new platform.
Write a Short Bio for space-limited platforms, a Standard Bio for most profiles, and an Extended Bio for platforms that allow more detail.
Scale the same message up smoothly across all three, adding more detail and context as space allows.
Having these ready keeps your identity consistent everywhere you show up.

## 06. Campaign Tracker Help

Campaign Tracker records your promotional pushes and major initiatives separately from everyday posting, so campaigns get the visibility they need.
For each campaign, note its goal, the platforms involved, start and end dates, and its status (planned, active, completed).
Distinguish time-bound campaigns from ongoing efforts, such as a seasonal promotion running May to August alongside an "ongoing" behind-the-scenes series.
This is what lets you see at a glance what's actively running and what's coming up next.

## 07. Analytics Tracker Help

Analytics Tracker is how you actually measure whether your social media efforts are working.
List your performance metrics, how often you'll report on them, and record insights, observations, trends, and improvement actions.
Turn data into real decisions: note, for instance, that "short-form video generates the highest engagement," then act on it by planning to "expand video-first content."
This is the step that turns your social media activity into something that actually improves over time.
