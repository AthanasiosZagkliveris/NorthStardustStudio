## 03. Revenue Model

**Structure:** Card List

**Number of Cards:** 2

**Cards:**
- Card 1: Revenue Stream

## 04. Revenue Matrix

**Structure:** Table

**Number of Columns:** 6

**Columns:**
- Column 1: #
- Column 2: Revenue Stream - Dynamic Field (Revenue Model: Revenue Stream)
- Column 3: Stream Mix (%) [Sum Check]
- Column 4: Revenue Method - Dynamic Field (Revenue Model: Revenue Method) [Repeatable]
- Column 5: Method Mix (%) [Repeatable Value] [Group Sum Check]
- Column 6: Category - Resources Field
