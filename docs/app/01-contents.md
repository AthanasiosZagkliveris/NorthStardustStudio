## How the app is entered

1. **Organisations** (home, `/`): the user's name (one box, no label: "Your name, as it should appear on your documents"; it starts the first founder's name on Founders & Ownership); create, import, export or delete an Organisation; a **Reload** button beside Add, Export and Import reloads the app and refreshes the saved examples. The two examples are in `src/data/examples/` (Lumen & Loom Studio, Quillfield Automation). Opening an Organisation opens **System Selection**. The **Capability Generator** is a future feature (owner, 2026-11-22): its page (`/generator`, one text box and a disabled "Analyse Capability") stays unlinked until it is built.
2. **System Selection** (`/assistant`), titled **Capabilities**: a tile for each Capability (Foundation, Value Delivery, Information, Organisation, Communication, Performance) in its colour, listing its Systems, its dialog a tile for each System (owner, 2026-11-22); a line "back to the last place worked" when there is one. In the Foundation, **Business Documents** (`/documents`, which opens the Workbook at its Business Documents). The Calendar opens from the Menu as a side panel at the left, in the Notes panel's style (owner, 2026-11-22).
3. **A System's pages** (`/introduction`, then `/conversation`): an Introduction, then its pages. The left rail lists the pages (Pages), the right rail the sections of the page (Sections), the page arrows go previous / next, and the arrow keys do the same where nothing else uses them. A page drawn as tiles has no Sections rail.
4. **Header** (with an Organisation open): the TILES mark at the left, the Organisation's name and the save status in the centre, then the **Menu** (owner, 2026-11-21; `ToolsMenu.tsx`: Search, Notes, Calendar, Focus on a System's pages, Settings, and Appearance, the reading mode), the **Workbook** button (2026-11-09: one button, no count, opening the Workbook page, below), and the Organisation's logo at the right end where it has one (owner, 2026-11-26). In the Menu: Search (Ctrl+K: pages, fields, the Glossary's terms and the answers), **Notes** (the user's own notes, in a panel on the right: "Notes" with its closing arrow, "+ Add note" under it, the open Organisation's notes with no heading and the others' in their groups; an empty note (no text, link or date) goes when it is left, when the panel closes and when the notes are read, a note linked to a page, card or field, "Link a field" picks it on the page; as before the Workbook, 2026-11-09: the card notes are the Workbook's), Settings (Global Settings). On the Organisations screen (the Landing page) the Menu holds the user's own Calendar and Appearance, and the Workbook's button is the **Handbook** (2026-11-22; the same page, `landing` in `Workbook.tsx`): App Guide, Specs & Limitations, Future Features, the Total review (every Organisation's Review), the Glossary of the app's terms alone, and the References.
5. **Other pages**: the **Workbook** (`/workbook`; its shape is layout rules section 7, "The Workbook"): one framed panel with arrows and a tab of bullets, its sections **Card Notes** (every card's Notes; a press opens the card; the user's own notes are the header's Notes tool), **Review** (Still open, Worth a second look, Key dates, Missing from the documents; "Go there"), **Glossary** (the app's terms in blue, the user's in terracotta, one funnel), **Business Documents** (Business Plan, Business Model Canvas) and **Financial Documents** (Financial Forecast, Funding Request, Financial Plan; `specifications/06-workbook/README.md`); from the Landing page, the **Handbook** (above), which alone holds the **References** (a Harvard list; a citation inside an Organisation opens them there); and Global Settings (`/settings`).

## The Foundation systems

| System | Pages | What it records |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Identity | One page, Identity System, of two chapters, Definition and Purpose (its tiles: `specifications/01-identity-system/01-definition.md`, section 1) | Who the organisation is in law and in purpose |
| 2. Brand | One page, Brand System: Ambassador (Aesthetics, Body Language, Communication & Voice, Imprint) and Style (Colours, Typography, Text Hierarchy, Icon & Logo, Brand Images) | How it looks |
| 3. Business | 1 Foundation & Engagement, 2 Infrastructure & Capability, 3 Financials | What it sells, to whom, with what, and whether the money works |
| 4. Strategy | One page, Situation & Direction: Current Situation Analysis, SWOT Analysis, Strategic Compass, Strategic Period (its dates, the Roadmap tile, the Theme cards with their Goals, Objectives and Metrics) | Where it is heading |

(The saved sections 2 Goals & Objectives and 3 Roadmap of the Strategy System, and the Founders Profile of the Identity System, are hidden pages: their data is kept and drawn inside the tiles above. The table of every tile by page is `design/foundation/01-current-design.md`, B.1.)

### Business, page by page
- **1. Foundation & Engagement**: one Segment at a time (the Segments rail on the right), each Segment a chapter, its two subchapters side by side in its one frame: Business Foundation and Engagement (owner, 2026-11-20 13:28 UTC+2: "Segment - chapter, Business Foundation, and Engagement - subchapters"). Business Foundation: for each customer Segment, four tiles: Segment Description, Value Proposition, Competitors, Offerings. The Segment card asks choice questions in five boxes (Demographics, Geographics, Business Data, Purchasing Power & Behaviour, Market Size & Industry Outlook; a Proxy or Dual-Intent buyer adds a User part), which follow what Identity and Positioning chose (Questions 12); under them stands the Profiles section, and a Profile asks its own questions (Questions 13). A Profile, a Competitor and an Offering are cards that open floating.
- **Engagement** (the second chapter of a Segment on 1. Foundation & Engagement): how each Segment finds, chooses and buys: three tiles: Channels & Journey (the Customer Channel; a channel can make cost cards: "+ Add expenses", typical costs), Customer's Relationship and Sales Forecast (tiles, Questions 17).
- **2. Infrastructure & Capability**: two cards of four tiles. Infrastructure: Founders, Resources, Activities, Partners & Suppliers. Offering's Capability: Resources, Activities, Partners & Suppliers, Delivery. Under them, in the footer's band beside the page's Back: Inventory (every card with its cost, as tiles or as a table with a column list and a filter and sort window that opens outward; text size Aa in three steps) and Completed (grey; only while a card is completed). The footer lets the pointer through to them.
- **3. Financials**: one chapter, Financial Foundation, its tiles and the records in the Workbook's Financial Documents in Questions 20, section 14. The title says the plan's months.

## Where figures and dates come from
- **The Opening Date** is set on the Identity System's Organisation tile; it decides the business stage, the plan start and the key dates (design Questions 2, 8.2a).
- **Global Settings** (header; sections Financial plan, Business stages, Pay & tax, Assets, Rates, Reserve, Channel Fee Table): the Plan Start (worked out) and plan length, tax, pay on-costs, capitalisation limit, Useful Life by group (and each asset's own years under its group), royalty and commission rates, reserves, the Channel Fee Table, and the Business stages (the Opening Date as a record, and the four durations: Planning, Launching, Managing, Growing).
- **The cards** carry their own dates, costs and statuses; the date defines the order of a list, then the number.
- **The Calendar** (left rail of System Selection) lists every card date (the key dates: plan start, registration, opening and the end of each stage's window; legal fees, starts, ends, warranty, payments, deadlines) with a funnel: the period, the kinds of date, the cards they come from; a note is made only on request.
- **Currency**: every total is in the organisation's currency (the registered country's); a card in another currency is listed and flagged, not counted.

## The pattern
Tiles open a dialog; a dialog lists card boxes; a card box opens a floating card; a card links to the cards it is connected to (R. Resource, A. Activity, P. Partner, S. Supplier). The arrows beside the last-opened card or section step through its list. A floating card of questions has one width, one content line and one double rule between its sections (`ai/09-layout-rules.md`, section 8); `npm run check:card` audits one.

## Where things are in the code
- Pages and editors: `src/components/interview/editors/*Editor.tsx`; the page shell `src/pages/Conversation.tsx`; the other screens in `src/pages/` (`Landing.tsx`, `SystemSelection.tsx`, `Workbook.tsx`, `GlobalSettings.tsx`, `ExportFlow.tsx`, `CapabilityGenerator.tsx`, and the Library's `Library.tsx`, `Inventory.tsx`, `Looks.tsx`); the routes are in `src/App.tsx`. Which sections make one page: `src/framework/engine/PageGroups.ts`; the System content: `src/assets/system-answers/`.
- Tiles, dialogs, floating cards, carousel: `src/components/interview/tiles/` (`SectionTiles.tsx`, `Tile.tsx`, `TileGrid.tsx`, `FloatingCards.tsx`, `DialogLevels.tsx`; the Inventory is `CapabilityOverview.tsx`), cards in `src/components/interview/cards/` (`CardBoxes.tsx`, `BusinessCardFields.tsx`).
- The Infrastructure & Capability editor (`editors/BusinessCapabilityEditor.tsx`) is a short component that calls five hooks in `editors/capability/` one after the other: `useCapabilityState` (writes, folds, reads of other pages), `useCapabilityCardFields`, `useCapabilityPeopleCards`, `useCapabilityActivityAndDelivery`, `useCapabilitySections` (the boxes and sections, which call one another); each gets what the ones before it returned and returns what the ones after it need.
- `AnswerRenderer.tsx` hands each Answer Structure to one function (`editors/answer/render*.tsx`, a `switch` on the type in `SingleAnswerRenderer`), each given one `scope` (the props with their defaults and the state of `useAnswerState.tsx`); add an Answer Structure by adding its function to a group file and a `case`.
- The other big editors are built the same way: the component calls hooks in a folder beside it (`editors/engagement/`, `editors/legal/`, `editors/foundation/`), and the constants and small helpers it had outside the component are in that folder's `shared.tsx`. In `editors/foundation/`, a Competitor's card and an Offering's card are files of their own (`CompetitorCard.tsx`, `OfferingCard.tsx`, 2026-11-09), called from `useFoundationSubsections.tsx` with what they read of their Segment (`FoundationCardScope`); one column of bands is `SingleColumn` (`shared.tsx`).
- The questions of Page 1's cards: `SegmentQuestions.ts` (the words and the rules), `SegmentContext.ts` (what Identity and Positioning settle for the Segment), `ProfileQuestions.ts`, `CompetitorQuestions.ts` and `OfferingQuestions.ts` (with the Value Proposition's draft and open items), and `EffectiveAnswers.ts` (the answers as the cards show them, with their starts, and what the later pages start from), all in `src/framework/engine/`; the cards are drawn by the hooks in `editors/foundation/`.
- Data shapes and readers, by domain in `src/framework/engine/`: `AnswerUtils.ts` (the generic part: defaults, emptiness, roles; it also re-exports all the others, so imports from it keep working), `AnswerProcess.ts` (process library and operational log), `AnswerBrand.ts`, `AnswerExport.ts`, `AnswerStrategy.ts`, `AnswerLegal.ts`, `AnswerFounders.ts`, `AnswerBusinessFoundation.ts` (Sections 1 and 2), `AnswerBusinessCapability.ts`; the money model: `FinancialModel.ts`; settings: `GlobalSettings.ts`; the business stage and its windows: `BusinessStage.ts`; key dates: `KeyDates.ts`; the Review: `ReviewItems.ts`; calendar dates: `CalendarCardDates.ts`.
- Styles: `src/styles/` (colours and sizes are tokens in `tokens/tokens.ts`, generated into `colors.generated.css` and `sizes.generated.css` (`npm run tokens`); tiles and cards in `section-tiles/` and `card-tile.css`; the global rules of a control in `cell-focus.css`). The shared controls are in `src/components/controls/` and each loads its own style.
- Examples: `src/data/examples/`. Rules for AI assistants: `docs/ai/README.md`.
