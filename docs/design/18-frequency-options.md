# The Frequency options

Status: **current design**, updated 2026-10-19.

Generated by `npx vite-node tools/scripts/catalogue/frequency-options.mjs` from the code (`BUSINESS_COST_FREQUENCIES`, the groups' lists in `AnswerUtils.ts`). Do not edit by hand.

## Changing the group or the category (2026-10-20)

A card that changes its category or its group starts its Frequency again: "Select" where the new group offers several, the one it offers where it offers one (its unit with it, a named "other" cleared, the price kept). Cards of Infrastructure (resources, partners and suppliers) and of Offering's Capability (resources, partners and suppliers) do the same (`costInGroup`).

## The default

The Frequency of a cost card (Resources, Partners & Suppliers, Delivery's Resources: Quantity, Price, currency, Frequency) offers **One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, Per unit, Per project, % of Rev** (seven). A group the lists do not know offers all seven. A group with one frequency shows it as text; a One-time one is not shown.

Other lists that are not this one:

| Where | Options |
|---|---|
| Legal expense (Cost, Frequency) | One time, Monthly, Annually |
| Strategy Metric (Frequency) | Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semiannually, Annually |
| Financial model (forecast lines) | One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, Per project, Per unit, Per order, % of Rev, Other |
| Founder (Salary, Frequency) | Monthly, Annually (and the others its card offers: see the Founders card) |

## The rules (2026-10-19)

1. **A card offers only what its group offers.** A frequency kept from another group, or set before the group was chosen, that the group does not offer is not listed: the card takes the group's first frequency (a group with one has it as text).
2. **A card with no group yet offers what the groups of its master tile offer, together** (Infrastructure's or Offering's), never one no group of it has.
3. **Per hour is a Per unit with the unit hour.** Every team group offers Monthly and Per hour: Admin Team (Infrastructure) now as Delivery Team (Offering) does.
