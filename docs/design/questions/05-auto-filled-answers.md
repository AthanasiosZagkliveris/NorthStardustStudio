**Kinds**
- **draft**: a text written in by itself (`useAutoDraft` / `AutoDraft`, src/components/interview/fields/useAutoDraft.ts) while it is empty or still holds the app's text. The Current Situation uses its own effect with a Refresh.
- **start**: a choice or figure shown from earlier answers until the founder sets it. It is not saved until then, or it is saved with the first thing written on that card.
- **options**: a list of options built or ordered from earlier answers.
- **auto card**: a card or row the app makes by itself from earlier answers.
- **computed**: a figure or line worked out and shown read-only where an answer stands.

**Doc abbreviations**
- "doc NN" is the file numbered NN in docs/design/ (foundation/, interface/, questions/, research/, records/ or library/).
- "spec Identity" is docs/specifications/01-identity-system/01-definition.md.
- "spec Brand" is docs/specifications/02-brand-system/01-brand.md.
- "Biz README" is docs/specifications/03-business-system/README.md, section "What crosses between the pages" and its table "What each later page reads from Section 1".
- "spec Channels", "spec Relationships", "spec P2", "spec Revenue", "spec Costs" are docs/specifications/03-business-system/02-channels.md, 03-customer-relationships.md, 04-infrastructure-and-capability.md, 05-revenue-and-pricing.md and 06-costs-funding-and-position.md.
- "spec Strategy" is docs/specifications/04-strategy-system/01-situation-and-direction.md.
- "spec GS" is docs/specifications/07-settings/README.md.
- "layout-rules" is docs/ai/09-layout-rules.md. Its rule "A draft is written in by itself" (section "The shared components") covers every auto draft generally.
