#### FRM.001 Frame

A rounded, coloured surface, wired or not, that carries information and tools. Its variants are the chapter, the tile (master, nested), the card (floating, nested) and the tag. Its corners are 8px.

**Variants**

##### FRM.CHP.001.001 Chapter

The frame of a chapter: its title, its fold arrow and its master tiles.

- What changes: The surface of a chapter; 1px edge.
- Height: follows the words
- Width: set by the layout
- Text size: no text (a symbol)
- Corners: 8 px; Border: 1 px; Padding left: 12 px; Padding top: 12 px

##### FRM.MTL.001.002 Master tile

The surface of a master tile: no edge, an inset shadow, 8px corners; 328px wide at 1920px.

- What changes: A tile of a chapter.
- Height: follows the words
- Width: set by the layout
- Text size: no text (a symbol)
- Corners: 8 px; Border: 0 px; Padding left: 13 px; Padding top: 1 px

##### FRM.NTL.001.003 Nested tile

The surface of a tile inside a dialog view (an open tile): 214px wide at 1920px.

- What changes: A tile inside a master tile's dialog.
- Height: follows the words
- Width: set by the layout
- Text size: no text (a symbol)
- Corners: 8 px; Border: 0 px; Padding left: 13 px; Padding top: 1 px

##### FRM.FLC.001.004 Floating card

The surface of a card that floats over the dimmed page: 492px wide, 12px padding, an inset edge and a shadow.

- What changes: A card over the page.
- Height: follows the words
- Width: set by the layout
- Text size: no text (a symbol)
- Corners: 8 px; Border: 1 px; Padding left: 12 px; Padding top: 12 px

##### FRM.NSC.001.005 Nested card

The surface of a card inside another card (Goal, Objective, Metric): it takes the width of the chapter and follows the card rules.

- What changes: A card inside a card; 1px edge.
- Height: follows the words
- Width: set by the layout
- Text size: no text (a symbol)
- Corners: 8 px; Border: 1 px; Padding left: 12 px; Padding top: 12 px

##### FRM.TAG.001.006 Tag

The surface of a page tag (the tab behind a rail): bullets only, a 37px tab.

- What changes: A tab at the side of a page or card. Not measured.
- Height: follows the words
- Width: set by the layout
- Text size: no text (a symbol)
- Corners: 8 px; Border: 1 px; Padding left: 12 px; Padding top: 12 px

### Layout frames (FRAME.LAY)

The page and the dialog view, the frames of the layout.

**What every component of this group has, besides the family's**
- Take the width of the page

#### FRM.002 Page

A page of a system: the header, the Pages rail, the page arrows and the chapters.

**Built from**
- FLD.PAG.106.001: moves between the pages
- ARW.PAG.001.001: moves to the next or previous page
- TTL.PAG.001.001: the title of the page

#### FRM.003 Dialog view

A view of tiles that opens over the page, with its own rail, arrows and a Back button.

**Built from**
- FLD.CAR.106.003: moves between the views or cards
- BTN.003: returns to the page
- TTL.DLG.001.003: the title of the dialog
