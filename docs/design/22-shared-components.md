# Shared components: which are shared, which are not

Status: current state on 2026-10-21, checked against the code of that day (`src/components/controls/`, `src/components/interview/`). This document describes the situation now. What changed, and when, is in the log (`04-log.md`); the short history at the end only says which round did what.

The aim of the owner: all similar things share ONE component, and no place draws its own markup for them (rule 22.24 of `docs/ai/layout-rules.md`). A place passes a class that says only where the thing stands and how wide it is; the look and the reactions belong to the component. The looks of the library agree with the app (`16-library-vs-app.md`).

A shared control loads its own stylesheet (it imports it), never only a page's sheet, because the Notes panel and the header are on every page.

## The cards, tiles and fields built on them

| Component | File | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `CardHead`, `NameCell`, `CardShell` | `interview/cards/` | The head of a folding card (fold arrow, number, mark, name, actions, summary), the name in it (asked, a link, or text), and the outer box of every card and section panel. |
| `BusinessCard` | `interview/cards/BusinessCardFields.tsx` | The shared card; with `legal` it draws the Legal Entity page's requirement, expense and founder cards (the CSS of the two stays separate on purpose: merging it moved the number and the glass ground). |
| `Tile`, `TileGrid`, `TileParts` | `interview/tiles/` | Every tile, every tile grid (the Inventory's is `bare`), and the shared parts: links layer, "+", hover origin. |
| `FieldRow`, `FieldHead` | `interview/fields/FieldRow.tsx` | A label with its control as a cell of the Legal Entity grid; `FieldHead` draws the label with "i", mark and status line for the Business fields too. |
| `DatePopup`, `ListStatic` | `interview/fields/` | The popup of the date cell and the month picker (`useDatePopup`); the read-only numbered or bulleted list. |

A card opened from another card that stays open stands below it as a pair (`StackUnderContext`, `DialogLevels.tsx`). The Inventory's card is alone.

## Still hand-made, awaiting the owner

Each needs a decision before it is replaced (the classes were found in the code on 2026-10-21):

- the answer-variant cards (`answer-variant-card`: a card chosen among several);
- the calendar's rail items, the landing cards, the tabs of the Business Documents, the picture boxes;
- `NumberedListInput` and `SuggestedInput` (editors with their own keys, not copies), the Strategy boxes' heads (`StrategyHead`, no fold arrow), the sub-channel head, the Brand column headings, the image-holder label (a layout rule measures it);
- the Process library's expand toggles and typed arrows (a page not reachable in this app version).

## Short history

- 2026-10-21, the Inventory window: `FoldArrow` (label) gets `direction` and `pressed` for the cost order; `RadioChoice` is also the table's text size; `OptionPicker` gets `keepWidth` and `tight`; tooltips use `tipWords`; the dropdown cell is one height.
- 2026-10-20: the sweep of hand-made controls (every x, add, row move, tool, text link and several-choice dropdown replaced); `RadioChoice` written; one fold arrow (`LabelArrow` merged into `FoldArrow`), one `NavArrow`, one `TextLink`; the native `title` tooltip replaced by `TitleTooltips`; the accent button is one rule set; `TickField`, `PopupPanel` and `FieldRow` written; `FoldHead`; `Tile`, `TileGrid`, `BusinessCard` for the Legal Entity cards.
- 2026-10-19: every dropdown is an `OptionPicker`; every one-line cell a `TextBox` or `NumberCell`; the free texts a `TextField`; `DatePopup`; `ListStatic`; `NameCell`, `CardHead`, `CardShell`; `SectionTiles.tsx` split into parts.
- 2026-10-17: the survey of the similar families (about 8 versions of a card, 15 text areas in 12 files, 106 plain inputs, 9 raw selects) that started the work.
