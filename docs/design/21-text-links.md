# The text links

Generated by `node scripts/catalogue/text-links-doc.mjs` from the code and the walk of the app. Do not edit by hand.

A text link is a piece of text that acts when pressed: it opens a card, a page or a list, or does something. All of them are drawn by ONE component (`TextLink.tsx`) and have one look (`src/styles/text-links.css`, the classes listed in `src/components/interview/textLinks.ts`): the default blue, regular, the letter spacing of a bold, no underline; under the pointer or the keyboard bold and underlined (the line 1px under the descenders); on one row, a longer text moving right to left under the pointer to be read whole. In a completed card they are grey until the pointer is on them.

The link symbol (a drawn chain beside a chosen dropdown name) and the kind letters (R., A., P., S.) are not text links: the symbol is a button in the card's tone, 20% larger under the pointer.
