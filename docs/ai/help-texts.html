Categories, groups and "i" texts

Pink = Infrastructure, brown = Offering.

The texts were checked by script: no negative wording and no repeated example or word across the 47 texts.

2. Partners and Suppliers (the categories play the part of groups)

TypePlaceGroupsText shown in the app
PartnersInfrastructureAlliances & NetworksOrganisations you join for the benefit of the whole company, e.g. Trade association, Buying group, Start-up accelerator, Producers cooperative.
SuppliersInfrastructureGoods & Equipment
Business Services
Creative & IT Services
Property, Vehicles & Facilities
Technical Support
  • Goods & Equipment: sellers of furniture, devices and consumables for the company, e.g. Furniture dealer, Stationery merchant, Computer reseller, Water-cooler rental.
  • Business Services: firms that run the administrative side, e.g. Accountant, Lawyer, Insurance broker, Payroll bureau, Business bank, Web host.
  • Creative & IT Services: freelancers and small agencies for design, branding, content or technology work, e.g. Web builder, Brand identity house, Photographer, IT support, Copywriter.
  • Property, Vehicles & Facilities: owners and caretakers of the premises, and lessors of vehicles, e.g. Landlord, Coworking operator, Cleaner, Car lessor.
  • Technical Support: outsourced technicians who maintain, repair and install the company's assets, e.g. Electrical engineer, Plumber, Boiler fitter, Network cabling. An installation is linked to the asset it installs.
PartnersOfferingRevenue Partners
Accreditation Bodies
  • Revenue Partners: parties who sell for you or send customers, and take a cut of each sale, e.g. Boutique stockist, Distributor, Referral partner, Affiliate blogger, Co-teacher.
  • Accreditation Bodies: bodies whose approval gives your work credibility, e.g. Weavers' guild, Craft standards panel, Culinary institute.
SuppliersOfferingProducers & Wholesalers
Logistics & Fulfilment
Contractors & Advisers
Agencies & Influencers
Venues & Organisers
Landlords, Leasing & Warehouses
Platforms & Payments
  • Producers & Wholesalers: producers and wholesalers that sell the materials and parts in the product, e.g. Wool mill, Box maker, Fabric importer, Flour miller.
  • Logistics & Fulfilment: carriers and warehouses that deliver orders to buyers, e.g. Courier, National post, Fulfilment centre, Freight forwarder.
  • Contractors & Advisers: outsourced specialists who do part of an offering's job or guide it, e.g. Dye works, Freelance developer, Product-safety adviser, Electrician.
  • Agencies & Influencers: firms and creators paid to spread the word, e.g. Marketing agency, Instagram creator, Poster press, Podcast sponsor.
  • Venues & Organisers: hosts of the places and events where you sell, e.g. Fair organiser, Exhibition hall, Show promoter.
  • Landlords, Leasing & Warehouses: owners of premises used for making or storing stock, and lessors of vehicles, e.g. Estate owner, Depot firm, Chilled-storage outfit, Van leasing specialist.
  • Platforms & Payments: intermediaries between you and the customer who charge a fee, e.g. Online marketplace, Payment processor, App store, Reservation portal.

3. Open point: group names that repeat a category word

A group name that repeats its category's word reads twice on screen. These still do it:

  • Utilities > Premises Utilities
  • Vehicles > Company Vehicles
  • Utilities > Process Utilities
  • Vehicles > Delivery Vehicles
  • Materials & Inventory > Materials & Stock
  • Partners > Revenue Partners
