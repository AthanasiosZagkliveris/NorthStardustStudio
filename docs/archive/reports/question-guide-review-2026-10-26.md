## 11. Conflicts found and decided (Identity and the Segment)

What each Identity answer gives the Segment is in doc 28, section 8.2 ("What the Segment will read"). The conflicts found while writing it:

(1) Doc 28 said both "several relationships: one Segment for each" and "multi-sided: one Segment in parts"; they could not both hold. Decided: one Segment frame for each relationship, a platform's sides included (a platform is a future feature, doc 33), and no parts. (2) The app's instruction text says "1 primary segment (maximum 2)" while the stage limits are one, one, two, any; the stage limit is the design, the instruction text is the owner's wording, and the owner decides whether it changes. (3) Mid-Market lies inside the official EU "medium" size (under 250 employees); the mapping of the owner's scales to the size bands is the author's.
