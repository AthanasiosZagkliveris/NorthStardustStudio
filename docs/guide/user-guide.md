# User guide: the app's tools and the Foundation

Status: **playbook**, written 2026-11-09, updated 2026-11-09. How a founder uses the app, step by step: the Organisations screen, the tools (Workbook, Notes, Search, Settings, Focus, the reading modes, the Calendar) and the Foundation with its Identity System and Business System.

Related: `app/contents.md` (the map of the app, for the team), `app/index.md` (every place in the app), `app/glossary.md` (the terms). The app's own help is the "i" beside a title or a question: it says what the question is for and how to answer it.

## 1. Before you start

The app helps you write down the decisions your business runs on: who you are, what you sell and to whom, what it costs, and whether the money works. You answer questions on cards; the app keeps every answer, adds up the figures and writes your documents from them.

- **Everything saves as you type.** The header shows "Saved" once an answer is kept. The answers stay in this browser on this device; export an Organisation to keep a copy or to move it to another device (section 2).
- **Every "i" is help.** Press the small "i" beside a title, a question or a tile to read what it means and how to answer it.
- **Back and Escape** return you to where you came from: the Back button at the bottom left of every page, and Escape closes the card, the dialog or the tool that is open.

## 2. The Organisations screen

The first screen lists your Organisations: each business you plan is one Organisation.

1. **Write your name** in the box at the top. It starts the first founder's name on the Legal Entity page and appears on your documents.
2. **Press "+ Add Organisation"** and type its name in the new row. Press a row to select it; the selected Organisation is the one you work on.
3. **Press "System Selection"** to start work on the selected Organisation.
4. **Keep a copy**: "Export Organisation" saves the selected one as a file; "Import Organisation" opens such a file, here or on another device. The x at the end of a row removes that Organisation, after you confirm.

Two examples come with the app, Lumen & Loom Studio and Quillfield Automation: open them to see a finished Foundation. The **Reload** button beside the reading mode reloads the app and refreshes the examples.

The header of this screen holds the reading mode and the **Workbook** (section 4.1), which here covers every Organisation.

## 3. The Foundation

### 3.1 Find your way in

1. On **System Selection**, press **Foundation** in the list of Capabilities. Its description opens; close it with Back or a press outside it. The Foundation's Systems show beside it.
2. Press **Identity System** (or another System). Its **Introduction** says what the System answers and lists its pages.
3. Press **Start Interview**, or a page in the Introduction's list, to begin.

The line "back to the last place worked" on System Selection takes you straight back to the page you left.

### 3.2 How a System's pages work

- **The Pages rail** at the left lists the System's pages. Each bullet shows the page's state: an empty circle (not started), a half-filled one (in progress), a filled one (defined). Press a bullet to go to that page.
- **The page arrows** above the rails go to the previous and the next page; the left and right arrow keys do the same.
- **The Sections rail** at the right lists the parts of the page; press one to scroll to it. A page drawn as tiles shows its tiles instead.
- **Tiles, dialogs and cards.** A page holds large tiles. Press a tile to open its dialog; a dialog lists the cards it holds and a "+" to add one; press a card to open it as a floating card beside the dialog. The arrows beside an open card step through the other cards of its list, and the bullets under them go straight to one.
- **A card that still lacks an answer** shows the field it lacks in red, with a short line saying why, and its tile flashes red until the answer is given.
- **Each card has a Notes field** at its end for your own remarks on that card. The Workbook gathers them (section 4.1).

### 3.3 The Identity System

Who the organisation is, in law and in purpose. Two pages.

**Page 1, Definition and Purpose**
1. **Organisation**: the name, the country, the legal form, the activities and the **Opening Date**. The Opening Date decides the business stage (Planning, Launching, Managing or Growing), the plan's start and the key dates, so set it first.
2. **Positioning** (the tile is named by your business stage, for example Early Positioning): who buys (B2C, B2B or both), the buyer's category and life stage, and, for a buyer who buys for someone else, the end user. Your answers here shape the questions of the Business System.
3. **Founders & Ownership**: each founder, their roles and what they bring: their industry, management, achievements and skills.
4. **Purpose & Beliefs**: three pair tiles, Mission & Vision, Values & Principles, Philosophy & Manifesto, each a short text in your own words.

**Page 2, Legal Entity**
1. **Capability**: the skills the team has and the ones it still needs.
2. **Registrations**: its two small tiles, **Registration Numbers** and **Additional Legal Requirements**, list what you have registered and what the law asks of your activity in your country.
3. **Expenses**: the legal costs (incorporation, trade marks, licences). Each becomes a cost card the Business System counts.

### 3.4 The Business System

What you sell, to whom, with what, and whether the money works. Five pages, best answered in order: each later page starts from what the earlier ones say.

1. **Foundation**: one frame for each customer **Segment**, with four tiles.
   - **Segment Description**: five boxes of choice questions about the people or businesses who buy, and under them the **Profiles**, each a typical buyer with their needs.
   - **Value Proposition**: what each Profile needs and how your offer answers it.
   - **Competitors**: who else serves the Segment, how they compare, and a SWOT Analysis.
   - **Offerings**: what you sell, its Description and its Terms & Service.
2. **Customer Engagement**: how each Segment finds you, chooses and buys. Add the **Customer Channels** you use; a channel can make its own cost cards ("+ Add expenses", with the channel's typical costs). Customer Relationship and Demand & Conversion follow as folding cards.
3. **Infrastructure & Capability**: two frames of four tiles. **Infrastructure**: Founders, Resources, Activities, Partners & Suppliers. **Offering's Capability**: Resources, Activities, Partners & Suppliers, Delivery. Each card carries its cost, its dates and its status. At the bottom, the **Inventory** lists every card with its cost, as tiles or as a table with a filter, and **Completed** holds the activities you have ticked as done.
4. **Financial Foundation**: Costs (a summary of what the other pages recorded), Funding Need, Capital & Funding and Financial Position.
5. **Sustainability**: Revenue and Break Even: when the business pays for itself.

### 3.5 Business Documents

The Foundation's answers write the **Business Plan**, the **Business Model Canvas** and the **Financial Forecast** (annual, quarterly or monthly). They are in the Workbook (section 4.1); the **Business Documents** button of the Foundation on System Selection opens them there. Each downloads as Markdown or PDF, and the Forecast as CSV too.

## 4. The tools

The tools stand in the header at the top of every page with an Organisation open. On a wide window each shows its word; on a narrower one, its icon, with its name in the tooltip.

### 4.1 Workbook

Everything you have written and everything the app explains, on one page.

1. Press **Workbook** in the header. The page opens on its first section, in a framed panel. Go round the sections with the arrows at its sides, or press a bullet in the tab beside it.
2. **Card Notes**: the Notes field of every card, page by page. The funnel chooses the pages; press a note to open its card.
3. **Review**: Still open, Worth a second look, Missing from the documents and Key dates. "Go there" opens the page or card an item is about.
4. **Glossary**: every term from A to Z, the app's words in blue (with their meaning and sources) and the names you gave in terracotta (with "Open the …"). The funnel chooses the kinds and the letter ranges.
5. **References**: every source the app cites, as a Harvard reference list. A citation in the Glossary opens its reference here.
6. **Business Documents**: the Business Plan, the Business Model Canvas and the Financial Forecast, a tab each (section 3.5).

On the **Organisations screen** the same button covers every Organisation: their Reviews, their terms in the Glossary (each named with its Organisation) and the References. Card Notes and Business Documents belong to one Organisation and show inside it.

Back or Escape returns you to where you opened the Workbook.

### 4.2 Notes

Your own notes, beside any page.

1. Press **Notes** in the header: the panel opens at the right and stays open as you move from page to page.
2. Press **+ Add note** and write. A note can carry a date, which puts it in the Calendar.
3. **Link a field** lets you point at a field, card or page: the note then opens that place, and the field shows a mark.
4. The open Organisation's notes come first; other Organisations' notes and the General notes are in their own groups below. A note left empty is taken away.

Press Notes again, the arrow beside the panel's title, or Escape to close it.

### 4.3 Search

Press **Search** or **Ctrl+K**, and type. The results come in four groups: **Pages**, **Fields**, **Glossary** and **Your answers**. Press a result to go there: a page opens, a field opens with its card unfolded, a term opens in the Workbook's Glossary.

### 4.4 Settings

Press **Settings** for the **Global Settings**: the rates and limits every calculated line uses. Change one and every figure that uses it follows.

- **Financial plan**: the plan's start (worked out from the Opening Date) and its length.
- **Business stages**: the Opening Date as recorded, and how long each stage lasts: Planning, Launching, Managing, Growing.
- **Pay and tax**, **Assets** (the useful life of each group), **Rates** (royalties and commissions), **Reserve** and the **Channel Fee Table**.

Each setting has its "i" with what it means.

### 4.5 Focus

On a System's pages, press **Focus** to read and write without distraction: the rails, the help and the header step aside and the page stays at its usual width. Press **Exit focus** at the top right, or Escape, to return. The app remembers your choice on this device.

### 4.6 Reading modes

The reading-mode picker, first among the header tools, sets how the app looks:
- **Light**: the standard look.
- **Sepia**: warm paper, for long reading.
- **Dark**: light text on a dark ground.
- **Auto**: follows your computer's light or dark setting.

### 4.7 Calendar

On **System Selection**, the **Calendar** tab in the left rail opens the calendar of the selected Organisation. It shows every date your cards hold (the plan's start, the registration and opening dates, the end of each stage, legal fees, payments, warranties, deadlines) and your dated notes.

1. Press a day to see what falls on it; press an item to open the page it comes from, or your note.
2. The funnel chooses the period, the kinds of date and the cards they come from.
3. To add a time or a few words to a date, point at it and press its note button; the app makes a note only when you ask for one. "Expand all" shows every note of the day.

## 5. A good order of work

1. Set your name, add the Organisation and choose the reading mode you like.
2. Identity System, page 1: the Organisation tile first (the Opening Date above all), then Positioning, then Founders & Ownership.
3. Business System, pages 1 to 5 in order: Segments and Offerings first, then the channels, the resources and activities, then the money.
4. Open the **Workbook's Review** after each session: it lists what is still open and what deserves a second look.
5. When the Review is clear, write the **Business Documents**, and export the Organisation to keep a copy.
